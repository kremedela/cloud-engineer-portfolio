============================================================
SC-300 — Configure & Analyze Cloud Discovery with MDCA
Full Detailed Instructions
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 — Enable Cloud Discovery
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to MDCA portal
2. Go to Cloud Discovery → Settings
3. Enable the Cloud Discovery feature

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/01-enable-cloud-discovery.png

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 2 — Connect Log Sources
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Choose log sources (firewall, proxy, endpoint)
2. Configure upload schedules or real-time collection
3. Validate connection

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/02-connect-log-sources.png

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 3 — Upload Traffic Logs
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Collect traffic logs from your environment
2. Upload logs into MDCA
3. Monitor processing status

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/03-upload-logs.png

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4 — Analyze Discovered Applications
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to Discovered Apps
2. Review app names, categories, and usage metrics
3. Identify unsanctioned or high-risk apps

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/04-analyze-apps.png

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5 — Identify Risky Applications
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Review risk score assigned by MDCA
2. Flag apps that violate company policy or security standards

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/05-risky-apps.png

------------------------------------------------------------
Step 6 — Generate Reports & Dashboards
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Export discovery reports
2. Create dashboards for executives and IT security
3. Use findings to implement access or block policies

📸 Screenshot: /screenshots/06-reports-dashboards.png

------------------------------------------------------------
End of Lab
============================================================
