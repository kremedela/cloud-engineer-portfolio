🧪 Lab Steps

Step 1 — Access Microsoft 365 Defender
Open your browser and navigate to https://security.microsoft.com
.
Sign in with your administrator account.

📸 Screenshot #1: Defender XDR dashboard (/screenshots/01-dashboard.png)

Step 2 — Review Security Reports
Go to Reports → Security Reports.
Review alerts generated over the past 7 days. Focus on high and medium severity alerts.

📸 Screenshot #2: Security Reports page (/screenshots/02-security-reports.png)

Step 3 — Analyze Threats
Select an alert to open the detailed report. Review affected users, devices, and attack vectors.

📸 Screenshot #3: Threat details (/screenshots/03-threat-details.png)

Step 4 — Respond to Threats
Use Actions → Investigate → Remediate to:

Quarantine infected devices

Reset compromised accounts

Block suspicious files or URLs

📸 Screenshot #4: Remediation actions (/screenshots/04-remediation.png)

Step 5 — Document Findings
Export the report as CSV or PDF for audit purposes.
Add notes for follow-up actions or recurring issues.

📸 Screenshot #5: Exported report (/screenshots/05-export-report.png)
