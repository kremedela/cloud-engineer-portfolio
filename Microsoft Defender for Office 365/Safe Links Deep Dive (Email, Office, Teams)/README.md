# 🛡️ Defender for Office 365 — Safe Links Deep Dive (Email, Office, Teams)

## 📌 Overview
This lab explores Safe Links protection across email, Office documents, and Microsoft Teams,
demonstrating time-of-click URL inspection and post-delivery protection.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Understand Safe Links time-of-click protection
- Configure Safe Links for email, Office, and Teams
- Analyze click activity and verdicts
- Validate URL rewriting behavior

## 🧰 Tools & Services Used
- Microsoft Defender for Office 365
- Microsoft 365 Defender Portal
- Microsoft Teams
- Threat Explorer

## 🏗️ Lab Structure
- Configure Safe Links policy
- Enable Office and Teams coverage
- Test malicious URLs
- Review click data

## 📝 Summary of Steps
- Create Safe Links policy
- Enable click tracking
- Validate time-of-click protection
- Investigate results

## ✅ Expected Outcome
Malicious URLs are blocked at time of click across email, Office apps, and Teams.
