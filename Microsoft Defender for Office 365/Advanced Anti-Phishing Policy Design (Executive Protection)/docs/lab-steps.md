# 🛡️ Advanced Anti-Phishing Policy Design (Executive Protection) — Lab Steps

## Plan Anti-Phishing Protection
1. Identify executive and high-risk users.
2. Document impersonation and spoofing risks.

📸 Screenshot: Executive user list.

---

## Configure Anti-Phishing Policy
1. Go to https://security.microsoft.com
2. Navigate to Email & collaboration → Policies & rules → Threat policies.
3. Create a new Anti-phishing policy.
4. Enable:
   - User impersonation protection
   - Domain impersonation protection
   - Display name spoofing detection
   - Mailbox intelligence

📸 Screenshot: Anti-phishing policy configuration.

---

## Assign Policy
1. Add executive users to protected users.
2. Set actions to Quarantine.
3. Enable safety tips.

📸 Screenshot: Policy assignment.

---

## Validate Protection
1. Send simulated impersonation email.
2. Verify detection in Threat Explorer.

📸 Screenshot: Threat Explorer results.

---

## ✔️ End of Lab
Executive impersonation attacks are detected and remediated automatically.
