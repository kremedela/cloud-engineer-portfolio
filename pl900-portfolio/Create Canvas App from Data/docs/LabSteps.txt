# 🧪 Create Canvas App from Data (Step-by-Step)

## Step 1 — Navigate to Power Apps
1. Go to **https://make.powerapps.com**
2. Select your environment from the top right.

📸 *Screenshot: Power Apps home screen*

---

## Step 2 — Start a New Canvas App
1. Select **Create**  
2. Under *Start from Data*, choose  
   - **Dataverse** table, OR  
   - **SharePoint** list, OR  
   - **Upload Excel**, OR  
   - **SQL Server**

📸 *Screenshot: Start from data selection screen*

---

## Step 3 — Choose Your Data Table
1. Pick the table/list containing your records.
2. Click **Connect**.

📸 *Screenshot: Table selection panel*

---

## Step 4 — Auto-generate the App
1. Power Apps will create:
   - Browse screen
   - Detail screen
   - Edit screen  

📸 *Screenshot: Auto-generated 3–screen layout*

---

## Step 5 — Customize the Browse Screen
1. Select the Gallery  
2. Modify:
   - Fields shown  
   - Sort order  
   - Search box behavior  

📸 *Screenshot: Editing the gallery fields*

---

## Step 6 — Configure the Detail & Edit Screens
1. Update the form layout  
2. Add/remove fields  
3. Enable validation  

📸 *Screenshot: Editing form fields*

---

## Step 7 — Test the App
1. Click **Play (▶️)**  
2. Test:
   - Browsing  
   - Viewing  
   - Editing  
   - Creating records  

📸 *Screenshot: Running the Canvas App*

---

## Step 8 — Save and Publish
1. Go to **File → Save**  
2. Click **Publish**  
3. Share with users  

📸 *Screenshot: Publish screen*

---

## ✔ End of Lab
Your app is now live and connected to real data.
