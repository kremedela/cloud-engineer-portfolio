# AI Builder Lab

## 🎯 Objective  
Use Microsoft Power Platform’s AI Builder to create, train, and integrate an AI model (form processing or prediction) into a Power App or Power Automate workflow.

## 🧱 Lab Structure  
- Part 1 — Create and configure an AI Builder model  
- Part 2 — Train and publish the model  
- Part 3 — Integrate the model into Power Apps or Power Automate  
- Part 4 — Validate and test output  

## 📝 Summary of Lab Steps  
1. Create an AI Builder model (Form Processing or Prediction).  
2. Upload and tag training data.  
3. Train and publish the model.  
4. Add the model to a Canvas App or Flow.  
5. Run test predictions or form extractions.  

## 📦 Prerequisites  
- Power Apps or Power Automate license that includes AI Builder credits  
- Access to Power Platform Admin Center  
- Sample images or training dataset  
- Environment with Dataverse enabled  

## 🔧 Tools and Services Used  
- Power Apps (Canvas App)  
- Power Automate  
- AI Builder  
- Dataverse  

## ✅ Expected Outcome  
You will successfully build, train, and integrate an AI model into a Power App or Flow, demonstrating practical AI automation capabilities in Power Platform.
