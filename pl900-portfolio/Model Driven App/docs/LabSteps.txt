# Model Driven App

## 📌 Prerequisites
- Dataverse tables created (from Lab 4)
- Dataverse RBAC configured (from Lab 5)
- Access to Power Apps Maker Portal

---

## ✅ Step 1 — Create a New Model-Driven App

1. Go to:  
   https://make.powerapps.com
2. Select your environment  
3. Click **Apps** → **New App** → **Model-driven app**
4. Name it:  
   **MM Operations Manager**
5. Choose **Modern App Designer**
6. Click **Create**

---

## ✅ Step 2 — Add Dataverse Tables

Inside the designer:

1. Click **Add → Pages**
2. Select:
   - **Table-based view and form**
3. Choose the tables:
   - IncidentTracking  
   - EmployeeDirectory (example)  
   - Assets (optional)
4. Click **Add**

---

## ✅ Step 3 — Configure the Sitemap Navigation

1. Select **Navigation**
2. Rename the main area:
   - **Operations**
3. Create group names:
   - **Incident Management**
   - **Employee Records**

4. Move tables under the correct groups:
   - IncidentTracking → Incident Management  
   - EmployeeDirectory → Employee Records  

5. Save

---

## ✅ Step 4 — Modify Views

1. Open **IncidentTracking → Views**
2. Edit the *Active Incidents* view:
   - Add columns:
     - Priority  
     - AssignedTo  
     - Status  
   - Add filter:
     - Status ≠ “Closed”

3. Save → Publish

---

## ✅ Step 5 — Modify Forms

1. Open **IncidentTracking → Forms**
2. Edit the main form:
   - Add sections:
     - *Incident Details*
     - *Assignment*
   - Add fields:
     - Title
     - Description
     - Priority
     - AssignedTo
     - Status

3. Save → Publish

---

## ✅ Step 6 — Add Dashboards (Optional but recommended)

1. Go to **Dashboards**
2. Create:
   - **Incident Overview Dashboard**
3. Add components:
   - Chart: Incidents by Status
   - Chart: Incidents by Priority
   - List: My Active Incidents

4. Publish

---

## ✅ Step 7 — Publish the Entire App

1. Click **Publish** (top right)
2. Click **Play** to validate functionality

---

## ✅ Step 8 — Secure the App with RBAC

1. Open the app settings  
2. Go to **Share**
3. Assign access to the role:
   - **MM Operations Analyst** (from Lab 5)
4. Verify only users with this role can see the app

---

## 🎉 End of Lab 6
You now have a fully working Model-Driven App for the MM Operations workflow.
