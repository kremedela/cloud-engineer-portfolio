# Dataverse RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)

## 📌 Prerequisites
- Access to a Power Platform environment  
- Dataverse database enabled  
- At least one **custom table** (from Lab 4)

---

## ✅ Step 1 — Create a New Business Unit
1. Go to **admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com**
2. Select your environment → **Settings**
3. Navigate to:
   **Users + Permissions → Business Units**
4. Click **New Business Unit**
5. Enter:
   - Name: **MM-Operations**
   - Parent BU: *Your root BU*
6. Click **Save**

📌 This Business Unit will be used to segment data access for users or teams.

---

## ✅ Step 2 — Create a New Security Role
1. Inside the same environment, go to:  
   **Power Apps → Tables → Settings → Security roles**
2. Click **New role**
3. Name it: **MM Operations Analyst**
4. Choose the **Business Unit**:
   - MM-Operations

This role will own permissions for the Operations users.

---

## ✅ Step 3 — Configure Table Permissions
1. Open the **MM Operations Analyst** role  
2. Go to the **Core** tab
3. Under table permissions, configure:

| Table | Read | Write | Append | Assign | Share |
|-------|------|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| IncidentTracking (your custom table) | Business Unit | Business Unit | BU | None | None |
| Contact | Organization | Read Only | Org | None | None |

4. Save the role

📌 This ensures they can only see records within their BU.

---

## ✅ Step 4 — Assign the Security Role to a User
1. Go to:  
   **Users + Permissions → Users**
2. Select a user (ex: test user)
3. Click **Manage Roles**
4. Assign:
   - **MM Operations Analyst**

---

## ✅ Step 5 (Optional) — Create a Team & Assign Role
1. Go to  
   **Users + Permissions → Teams**
2. Click **New Team**
3. Enter:
   - Name: **Ops Analysts Team**
   - Type: **Security**
   - Business Unit: **MM-Operations**
4. Save

5. Open the team → **Security roles**
6. Assign:
   - MM Operations Analyst role

📌 Any user added to this team inherits the role automatically.

---

## ✅ Step 6 — Testing the Role
Log in as the assigned user:
- Verify you **can see** only BU matching records  
- Verify you cannot **modify**, **delete**, or **assign** outside your permissions  
- Ensure records from other BUs are hidden
