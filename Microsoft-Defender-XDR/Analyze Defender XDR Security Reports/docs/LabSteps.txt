# Lab: Analyze Defender XDR Security Reports

## Step 1 — Access Security Reports
1. Log into https://security.microsoft.com
2. Navigate to **Reports → Security reports**
3. Review available reports:
   - Alert summary by category
   - Incident trends
   - Detection sources
4. Focus on reports with **High** and **Medium** severity alerts

📸 Screenshot #1: Security reports dashboard
(/screenshots/01-security-reports.png)

---

## Step 2 — Analyze Trends
1. Review alert trends over the last 7–30 days
2. Identify:
   - Most frequent alert categories
   - Sources of recurring alerts (Endpoint, Email, Identity, Cloud)
   - Any spike in specific alert types

📸 Screenshot #2: Trend analysis chart
(/screenshots/02-trend-analysis.png)

---

## Step 3 — Identify Patterns and Recurring Alerts
1. Click into each alert type
2. Review:
   - Affected entities
   - Correlated incidents
   - MITRE ATT&CK mapping
3. Note any recurring issues that may require remediation or policy changes

📸 Screenshot #3: Alert pattern identification
(/screenshots/03-recurring-alerts.png)

---

## Step 4 — Prioritize Areas for Remediation
1. Rank findings based on:
   - Severity
   - Impact on users/devices
   - Frequency of occurrence
2. Create a short remediation plan
3. Include which policies, endpoints, or users need attention

📸 Screenshot #4: Prioritized remediation plan
(/screenshots/04-remediation-plan.png)

---

## Step 5 — Document Recommendations
1. Add notes in Defender portal or internal SOC tracker
2. Include:
   - Identified risks
   - Suggested mitigations
   - Responsible teams
3. Ensure findings are actionable
